THE OLD AGENT, IN ONE PICTURE.

agent.py (GTPS-Agent) tried to be a governed partner:
- clause text or skills stuffed into the window ('behave like this')
- file: often went through a picker of passages, not always the whole file
- a second voice (Proxy) often sat in the chair, or its notes were saved as if they were the answer
- extra meters (fatigue, health, 'clear') spoke a lot and went numb
- continuity was partly a hope that the model 'still had the thread'

A lot of machinery between you and the sentence.

THIS PROGRAM, IN ONE PICTURE.

run.py is you and one model, plus a clerk who does not speak in the answer.

  you type
      → the model answers   (the conversation — the main window)
      → one clock line      FETCHED: none  or  FETCHED: /the/file
      → the clerk files the minutes  (you do not)

No clause pack. No Proxy as the face. No picker on file:. No dashboard.
/shape is a second glance at form. That glance is not the next answer.

A BARE TURN. you @ turn 1 › What did we say about the backup last week?

You get an answer. The clock says FETCHED: none.
Read that as: nothing from disk was placed; this is the model talking. Same kind of fact as 'no attachment on this email.' Not a scolding.
The model may wander. Say 'one sentence only' or /forget (diary kept).

EVIDENCE IN THE ROOM. When it must be this document:

  you @ turn 2 › file: /home/schnee/notes/backup.txt What did we decide?

The whole file is placed, or the program refuses. Clock: FETCHED: that path.
The model can still ignore the file. Now you can see the mismatch.
You are not hoping a retriever chose the right paragraph. You pointed.

THREE JOBS. Only one is the chat.

Talk / meaning — the model — type questions; read the answer as talk.
Look-up — you + file: — when it must be a real document; watch the clock.
Minutes — the clerk — nothing. The diary writes itself. /views if curious.

The old agent tried to make the model do all three at once. That is why it felt like sparring.
Here: meaning stays in the talk; evidence enters when you place it; memory that does not fade into the weights is the diary.
Benefit: file: when the sentence will matter; believe the clock more than a confident tone.

WHAT THIS WILL NOT FEEL LIKE.

Not a nanny. Not three personas introducing themselves. Not a disclaimer on every line.
It will feel quieter. Put the file in when it counts, read the answer, glance at FETCHED:. That is the engagement.
If you treat every confident paragraph as 'looked up,' you are back in the old vortex — this program cannot stop that, it can only make the miss visible.
